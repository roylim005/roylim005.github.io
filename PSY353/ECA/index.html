Well-being study instruments

Interview notes and three short well-being scales for the PSY353 ECA. Everything stays in this browser until you download it as JSON. Nothing is sent to a server.

Interview notesFor the interviewer. Sixteen questions with probes, notes and quotes. Satisfaction With Life Scale5 statements, about 1 minute. Flourishing Scale8 statements, about 2 minutes. PERMA-Profiler23 questions, about 5 minutes.

Give the scales after the interview, in the order listed. On a shared device, download each JSON and then clear the answers.
