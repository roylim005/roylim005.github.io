## 1. What Has to Get Done (Workscope)

| Component | Marks | What's Needed |
|---|---|---|
| **Q1a — 10-slide deck** | 45 | Campaign built on TPB, grounded in interview findings + academic research, with explicit mapping of attitudes/subjective norms/perceived behavioural control → intention → behaviour. Interview demographics table must appear in the slides. |
| **Q1b — 2–3 min video** | 40 | Promotional video for ONE persona (50yo or 60yo — TBC by group), addressing their specific barrier, covering both basic + cancer screening relevant to them. |

**Persona decision needed first (Week 1):** 50yo "not overweight so no need" OR 60yo caregiver who finds cancer screening taboo. This decision drives who you look for in interviews (people who resemble that mindset are the most useful data) and the tone of the video.

## 2. Roles (5 People)

| Role | Person | Core Responsibilities |
|---|---|---|
| **A — Interview Lead** | Person w/ existing contacts | Recruits 5 eligible interviewees (40+, no screening in 3 yrs), schedules, conducts interviews (can pair with others), keeps consent/anonymity in mind |
| **B — Research Lead** | Person strong in research | Sources 5+ peer-reviewed academic references (TPB literature, HSG/Singapore screening stats, health psych models), keeps APA reference list live from Week 1 |
| **C — Slide Deck Lead** | — | Synthesizes interview data + research into the 10-slide Q1a narrative; builds the TPB construct-mapping; owns the demographics table |
| **D — Video Lead** | Person good in video editing | Writes persona-specific script/storyboard, films, edits final 2–3 min cut |
| **E — Group Lead / QA** | — | Coordinates GBA declaration form numbering, cover pages, referencing consistency, final proofread, submission logistics |

**Notes:**
- All 5 should each help conduct **or** sit in on at least one interview — it spreads the workload and everyone understands the raw data firsthand (useful for the declaration form's "contribution" evidence too).
- Role C (slides) and Role B (research) should sync directly — the TPB mapping only works if research and interview findings are pulled together, not stapled on separately.
- Role D (video) can start storyboarding as soon as the persona is picked (Week 1), even before interviews wrap, since the persona is scripted, not interview-dependent.

## 4. Things Worth Flagging (easy to miss)

- **Interview anonymity:** you can skip names, but the demographics table (age, gender, race, profession, education) is required — decide early how you'll record this without identifying anyone.
- **Persona ≠ interviewees:** your 5 interviewees don't have to match the chosen persona exactly, but if their concerns overlap with the persona's, your campaign argument gets stronger — worth being intentional about who Person A recruits.
- **GenAI disclosure:** if anyone uses AI for brainstorming/outlines, the brief requires disclosure via citation/table — decide as a group how you'll track this so it's consistent across all 5 of you.
- **Video length is a hard cutoff** — anything past 3 minutes isn't graded, so build in a trim/edit buffer, not just a "final cut" deadline.
- **Declaration form numbering** must be *identical* across all 5 submissions (same person = same number for everyone) — agree on the list once, in writing, in Week 1, so nobody's form conflicts.
- **Version history / source PDFs:** the brief recommends keeping Google Docs/Word Online version history and a zipped folder of cited source PDFs as plagiarism-flag insurance — worth setting up a shared Drive folder from day one rather than scrambling later.
- **In-text citations must fit within the 10 slides** (only the reference page is exempt) — Slide Lead and Research Lead should agree on citation format/shorthand early so it doesn't eat slide space in Week 4.
